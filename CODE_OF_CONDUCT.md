# Code of Conduct

Berea is a project rooted in Christian values, and we want the community around it to reflect that: charitable, honest, and welcoming — including to people exploring the faith, asking hard questions, or coming from a different tradition than you.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, especially when discussing contested theological or denominational topics
- Assume good faith in disagreements over canon, translation, or interpretation
- Give constructive, specific feedback on PRs and issues
- Cite sources when disputing content accuracy

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Deliberately misrepresenting a text's canonical status or historical origin to push a personal agenda
- Dismissing contributors based on their denomination or tradition
- Spam or off-topic promotion

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official Berea community channels.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainers (contact details to be added once the repo is public). Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation for violations of this code.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
