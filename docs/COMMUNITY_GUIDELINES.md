# Berea Community Guidelines & Moderation Policy

Welcome to Berea. Our community exists to foster unhurried, reverent scripture study, fellowship, and prayer across historic Christian traditions (Protestant, Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, and Ethiopian Orthodox).

To preserve a peaceful and respectful environment, all participants must follow these guidelines.

---

## 1. Core Principles

- **Reverence & Respect**: Treat Holy Scripture and fellow believers with dignity, charity, and grace.
- **Graceful Dialogue**: Theological differences across traditions should be discussed with humility and academic/spiritual curiosity, avoiding hostile argument.
- **No Harassment or Hate Speech**: Denigrating comments based on race, ethnicity, nationality, tradition, gender, or personal identity are strictly prohibited.
- **Spam & Self-Promotion**: Commercial advertising, repetitive spamming, or self-promotional link dropping is not permitted.

---

## 2. Posting Policy & Anonymity

- **Anonymous Option**: Believers may post prayer requests anonymously to protect personal privacy regarding sensitive personal or family needs.
- **Real-Name / Display Name**: When posting non-anonymously, display names should represent genuine study identities.
- **Rate Limits**: To prevent spam floods, users are limited to 3 new prayer posts per hour.

---

## 3. Moderation & Reporting Workflow

1. **Flagging**: Any member can click **"Report"** on a prayer post or comment to flag it for moderation review.
2. **Review Queue**: Reported content enters the Admin Moderation Queue.
3. **Action & Enforcement**:
   - **Dismiss**: If content complies with guidelines, the flag is dismissed.
   - **Archive / Hide**: Inappropriate content is hidden or archived.
   - **User Mute / Block**: Members can block specific users to hide all future posts from them locally.
   - **Account Restrictions**: Repeat offenders will be banned from public posting.
